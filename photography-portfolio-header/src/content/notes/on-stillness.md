---
title: "On stillness"
date: 2024-09-12
---

There is a difference between a scene that is quiet and a scene that has become quiet. The first is simply still. The second carries the memory of movement — a chair pushed back, a window left open, a light left on.

Most of what I photograph belongs to the second kind. The stillness is the subject, but the question underneath is: what was here before?
