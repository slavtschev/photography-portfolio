---
title: "The Ordinary and the Unseen"
date: 2024-06-20
excerpt: "I am not trying to make the ordinary look extraordinary. I am trying to show that it already is."
---

I am not trying to make the ordinary look extraordinary. I am trying to show that it already is.

There's a tendency in photography — and I've fallen into it myself — to chase the exceptional. The golden hour. The decisive moment. The perfectly composed frame. These things are worth pursuing. But they can train you to overlook what's actually in front of you.

The most interesting photographs I've taken were not planned. They happened in corridors, on stairwells, waiting for something else. A particular quality of light on a wall I'd walked past a hundred times. A person doing something completely unremarkable, with complete absorption.

The camera is a tool for paying attention. You don't need to go anywhere special. You need to look more carefully at where you already are.
